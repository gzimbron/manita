Manita

A cartoon hand for your pointer

A tiny macOS menu bar app that swaps your cursor for a cartoon foam hand with a sleeve, a click bounce and a natural click sound.

brew install --cask gzimbron/tap/manita

Pointer mood

Four stops from Subtle to Look at me!

Presentation mode

⌃⌥T holds clicks back while the hand keeps poking.

Works across displays

The sleeve follows you onto the next monitor.
